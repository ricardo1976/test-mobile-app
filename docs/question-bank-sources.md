# Initial question bank sources

This catalogue records reference sources used to check the Spanish-first
question bank. The question bank contains concise original prompts and
paraphrased facts; it does not reproduce source text.

## Television and film

- RTVE Play archive collections: [1980s](https://www.rtve.es/play/colecciones/play-archivo-los-80/234/), [1990s](https://www.rtve.es/play/colecciones/play-archivo-los-90/235/), and [2000s series](https://www.rtve.es/play/colecciones/series-de-los-2000/1886/).
- Spanish Film Academy, [100 Spanish films students should know](https://www.academiadecine.com/2020/03/25/el-cine-entretiene-y-educa-las-100-peliculas-espanolas-que-todo-estudiante-deberia-conocer/).
- RTVE Play, [Spanish films from the 1990s](https://www.rtve.es/play/colecciones/peliculas-anos-90/3954/).

## Music

## Video games and technology

## Everyday culture

- European Central Bank, [the euro](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/intro/html/index.en.html) and [the 2002 cash changeover](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/changeover/2002/html/index.en.html).
- RTVE, [1990s summer songs and popular culture](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20080718/las-canciones-del-verano-en-los-90/117551.shtml).

The sources provide historical reference material for fact checking; the
maintainer should add a more specific source link when correcting or adding a
question whose date or regional release differs by market.
